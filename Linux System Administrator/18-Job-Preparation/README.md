## 💼 Resume Tips

```
✓ DO:
✓ 1-2 pages में fit करो
✓ Achievement-focused होना चाहिए
✓ Quantifiable results दो (not "managed servers" but "managed 50 servers")
✓ Relevant skills highlight करो
✓ ATS-friendly format (bullet points, standard fonts)
✓ Updated होना चाहिए (हर 6 महीने में)

✗ DON'T:
✗ Photo न लगाओ (India में अब optional है)
✗ Personal details न दो (spouse, children)
✗ Salary expectations न लिखो
✗ Long paragraphs - bullet points use करो
✗ Irrelevant information (school details, old projects)
```

## ✍️ DAY 4: Cover Letter Templates

```

position at [Company Name]. As a recent graduate with a passion for 
Linux systems and cloud technologies, I am excited to contribute to 
your team.

During my academic projects and internship at [Previous Company], I 
have developed strong skills in:
• Linux system administration and troubleshooting
• Shell scripting and process automation
• Cloud infrastructure (AWS/Azure)
• Monitoring and alerting systems

I am eager to learn and grow with your team, and I am confident that 
my technical foundation and dedication will make me a valuable team member.

Thank you for considering my application.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```
