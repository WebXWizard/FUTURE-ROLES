# Answer Framework

Use this structure for analytics answers:

```txt
Problem -> Data -> Method -> Result -> Business recommendation -> Limitation
```

Example:

```txt
The business wanted to reduce customer churn.
I analyzed customer activity, subscription history, complaints, and renewal data.
I cleaned missing values, segmented users, and compared churn by usage, plan, and support tickets.
The strongest churn signal was low product usage in the first 30 days.
I recommended onboarding reminders and early success tracking.
```
