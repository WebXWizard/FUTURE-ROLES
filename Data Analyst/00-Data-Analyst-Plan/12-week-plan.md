# 12 Week Data Analyst Plan

## Weeks 1-2

- Learn data analyst role, KPIs, Excel formulas, pivot tables, and charts.

## Weeks 3-4

- Practice SQL joins, aggregations, filtering, grouping, and window functions.

## Weeks 5-6

- Learn Python, pandas, data cleaning, notebooks, and exploratory analysis.

## Weeks 7-8

- Study statistics, probability, hypothesis testing, and dashboard design.

## Weeks 9-10

- Build Power BI or Tableau dashboards and business analytics projects.

## Weeks 11-12

- Prepare portfolio, case study explanation, interview questions, and resume points.
