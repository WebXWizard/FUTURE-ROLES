- Revise ML lifecycle, Python, Linux, Git, and environment management.

## Weeks 3-4

- Learn Docker, container images, cloud basics, and artifact storage.

- Add CI/CD, automated tests, monitoring, drift detection, and retraining triggers.

## Weeks 11-12

- Build portfolio projects, revise Kubernetes, governance, and interview scenarios.
