# 12 Week Data Science Engineer Plan

## Weeks 1-2

- Learn data science role, Python, SQL, statistics, and data cleaning.

## Weeks 3-4

- Practice EDA, visualization, feature engineering, and data pipelines.

## Weeks 5-6

- Learn regression, classification, clustering, and model evaluation.

## Weeks 7-8

- Study deep learning, NLP or vision basics, experimentation, and A/B testing.

## Weeks 9-10

- Learn big data, cloud analytics, deployment, and MLOps basics.

## Weeks 11-12

- Build portfolio projects, revise interviews, and prepare resume points.
