# AI ML Engineer Study Plan

AI ML Engineers build models and AI systems that solve real problems.

Focus on:

- ML fundamentals
- Python, math, and statistics
- Supervised learning
- Unsupervised learning
- Deep learning
- NLP, LLMs, and generative AI
- Computer vision
- Model evaluation
- Deployment and MLOps basics
- Responsible AI
- AI system design

Suggested order:

1. AI and ML fundamentals
2. Python, math, and statistics
3. Supervised learning
4. Unsupervised learning
5. Deep learning
6. NLP and LLMs
7. Computer vision
8. Evaluation and optimization
9. Deployment and responsible AI
10. Projects and mock interviews
