# 12 Week AI ML Engineer Plan

## Weeks 1-2

- Learn AI, ML, Python, math, statistics, and data preprocessing.

## Weeks 3-4

- Practice regression, classification, tree models, metrics, and feature engineering.

## Weeks 5-6

- Study clustering, dimensionality reduction, neural networks, and deep learning basics.

## Weeks 7-8

- Learn NLP, embeddings, transformers, LLMs, prompt engineering, and RAG basics.

## Weeks 9-10

- Study computer vision, model optimization, deployment, and MLOps basics.

## Weeks 11-12

- Build portfolio projects and prepare mock interviews, system design, and resume points.
