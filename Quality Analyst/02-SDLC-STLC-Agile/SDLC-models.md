# SDLC Models

SDLC means Software Development Life Cycle.

Common models:

- Waterfall: sequential phases, useful when requirements are stable.
- V-model: testing activities map to development phases.
- Iterative: product improves through repeated cycles.
- Agile: product is built and tested in short iterations.
- Spiral: risk-driven model for complex systems.

QA involvement:

- Review requirements early.
- Identify ambiguity.
- Plan tests before development finishes.
- Validate business flow during execution.
- Give release confidence through evidence.

Interview line:

```txt
QA should be involved from requirement analysis, not only after development is complete.
```
