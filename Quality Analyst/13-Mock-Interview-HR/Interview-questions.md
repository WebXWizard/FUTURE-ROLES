# Interview Questions

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why QA?
3. What is your QA project?
4. What tools have you used?
5. How do you test with unclear requirements?
6. How do you handle pressure before release?
7. How do you work with developers?
8. What is your biggest QA learning?
9. What is your strongest testing skill?
10. What is your weakness?
11. Where do you see yourself in two years?
12. Why should we hire you?
