# Project And HR Round

Project answer structure:

```txt
Project goal -> Features tested -> Test approach -> Tools used -> Defects found -> Result -> Learning
```

HR questions:

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why do you want to work as a Quality Analyst?
3. What are your strengths?
4. What is your weakness?
5. How do you handle conflict with developers?
6. How do you manage deadlines?
7. Tell me about a time you found an important defect.
8. Why should we hire you?

Strong HR theme:

- You are detail-oriented, communicative, risk-aware, and focused on product quality.
