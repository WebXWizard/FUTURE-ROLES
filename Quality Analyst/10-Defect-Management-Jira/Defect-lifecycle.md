# Defect Lifecycle

Common defect states:

- New
- Assigned
- Open
- Fixed
- Retest
- Verified
- Closed
- Reopened
- Rejected
- Deferred

Severity vs priority:

- Severity means technical or user impact.
- Priority means how soon it should be fixed.

Examples:

- High severity, high priority: payment failure in production release.
- High severity, low priority: crash in rarely used old browser not supported soon.
- Low severity, high priority: company logo wrong on homepage before launch.
